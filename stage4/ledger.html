About this experiment

The geometry comes from measured forest scans. Species placement, seed routes, prices, fire and growth are teaching models.

One turn represents six months. Canopy growth is deliberately accelerated. The credits are not carbon credits or real market prices.

A closed pioneer stand holds the grass back. Native seeds can add other trees where routes remain open. This is a simplified game rule, not a claim that all birds or bats avoid open ground.

Every repeat clearance loses 2 health through damage to native regrowth, up to 8 per patch. Mature forest recovers this loss by 0.5 each turn. These are authored tradeoffs, not measured biodiversity values.

Changing the payment rule starts a fresh experiment. Undo and the turn history let you compare choices. This trial does not change Cooperation or Negligence.
