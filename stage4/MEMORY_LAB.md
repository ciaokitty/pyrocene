# Memory map lab

Open `memory.html` through the Stage 4 local server. It requires `living-landscape.mjs`; the lab fixes every team to `worldAtDay(3)`, the closing field-day scenario snapshot. It stamps both the snapshot day and the living-landscape version into every export, and rejects maps from another snapshot. If that declared training-world module is absent, the lab stops with a clear message rather than substituting mock values.

Teams make only their own recollections. A blank cell stays unknown and receives a neutral modelling assumption, not a damp firebreak. The fixed Day 3 comparison world is declared training data and is revealed only after commit. The 60 by 60 educational arrival display uses a compact port of the Stage 4 Rothermel surface-rate calculation, with fuel classes inferred from team fuel, moisture, and remembered exposure. It is a 20-minute teaching comparison, not native ForeFire and not a calibrated historical reconstruction. Every team uses the same ignition and weather. The score combines coverage and fuel-moisture reconstruction with timing of the predicted fire front.

Exported committed maps can be loaded for a group comparison. Optional photos remain local to the browser tab and require manual tracing. Optional 2023 history is a separate measured satellite layer, not a fire path or validation target.
