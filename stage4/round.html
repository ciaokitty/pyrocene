About this game

Measured forest points. Invented patches, budgets and recovery. Credits are game money, not carbon credits.

Forest health is a teaching score out of 100. Removal subtracts points for native damage. Restoration adds points as cover returns. It is separate from money and fire area.

The fire is simulated, not matched to a historical burn scar. Both runs use the same spark and wind. Recovery assumes successful care and wetter litter. This is not a forecast.

The room starts with 2 credits. Removal has a cost and a return. Shared sites reduce restoration costs. Going back to Cooperation or Expedition resets the shared game for both teams.

Links last until the server restarts. Projected growth reuses measured canopy points, not species-specific growth models.
