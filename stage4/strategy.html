YOUR MOVE

Keep the young forest alive.

In this stage, you control both removal and restoration.

There is a "ledger" of unstable plots at the top of the screen. This shows all plots that have been cleared, but where the canopy hasn't closed. You need to be more mindful of these or the effort put into restoring them can be wasted. Also remember: a restored patch is more resilient to fire.

Each turn is a 6 month cycle, and you must restore the forest in 12 years. You are given a starting grant of 12 credits. Try and be strategic about your approach.

A crew visit costs 1 credit, even if it finds nothing. Replanting costs 6 credits. Close view lets you inspect a square for free.

About this experiment

Aim to close five new canopies in twelve years. Strategies are advice, not different rules. Weather stays the same when you replay this landscape.

Measured forest scans provide the points. Plant placement, growth, budgets and fire are simulated. Six-month turns compress restoration for play; they are not predictions. Credits are game money, not carbon credits.

In this scenario, restored closed canopy blocks fire. Dense invasive fuel can scorch the edges of untouched forest. This simplified rule does not mean real closed-canopy forest is fireproof.

Forest health is a game score out of 100. It combines native canopy, invasive cover and fire damage. Growth and scar recovery improve it; money is counted separately.
