If someone is stuck

Ask what they are curious about. Open Map to help them choose a place, then try Close view. No mission blocks another. The paper exercise is always available.

Before handing out the map

Close the forest screens. Ask each person to name one place they remember. Then bring those recollections together on paper.

Bring back the earlier stages

Which small patches joined up? What did the room miss while voting? What did the fire lab show about breaks? Which observations in the films helped you decide where to look?

Include people

Who uses these places? Where could coordination prevent an escape? What would a fire cost beyond the trees? What would a carbon project still need to demonstrate?

When memories conflict

Keep both accounts and mark uncertainty. Ask what each person observed. A remembered colour is not the same as a field moisture check.
