This documents some gameplay assistance you are allowed to think about for the participants. 
For the problem of building new pieces to the board that are realistic you are allowed to look at all online sources including earth engine (~/.config/earthengine/credentials and the earthengine cli should work). 

During gameplay you are also allowed to give the user an "ai assist" if you feel it can really help them with the experience of finding the right gaps. Meaning if the problem of where do i even deploy these sensors in a real rainfores landscape seems too daunting - we can have them chat with an in game assistant that helps them understand a few key concepts and then positions the data collection and hypothesis. To try this you can use the openrouter key (~/.config/idlisseus/openrouter.json) and a qwen 3.8 27b model. You may even use a local test rig that just accepts chats from the user and uses you (codex) to answr to save cost during testing. The main idea is to just have an in game assistant. This assistant could be a continuous chat bot or even someone who interrupts gameplay to give advice/assistance like in metal gear solid Mei Ling. This assistant would show up once at the start of the game maybe, and then keep showing up based on what the user is doing to help them make decisions. For example if they deploy a hyperspectral drone they may see something thats quite complicated and the assistant would show up asking if they would like to classify it for patterns. 

We would make this assistant not too open ended. Like i don't want participants having conversations about all sorts of things with it, nor about open ended fire topics. It would just tell them about a few select game play things. I don't think voice or sound is needed as this will be in an open room. Just text. Of course it may be possible to do this assistant as a simple non ai character too and that would be simpler if possible - not sure whats the best approach here. 

We're not going for a long game, maybe a maximum of 20m, minimum of 10m. 

Another cool assist could be like a "pokedex" kind of thing. So in the cinematic map we see different colors and the user can ask aobut one of those canopy colors and get a reading on the treedex for info about that tree, is it native, is it dry etc.

Or maybe they can look for what stack of things are always found around fire signatures and ask for that classification. Youll have to play around.
