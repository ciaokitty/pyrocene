## Things that would be easy to get wrong later

**Do not un-freeze the engine copy.** `stage2/engine/` looks like duplication
and is not. The isolation test exists to stop somebody tidying it away.

**Do not balance hunting and resilience into parity.** See the balance entry.
The asymmetry is the lesson. If it needs changing, change how rooms lose, not
how the ecology works.

**Do not let the trench go near water.** It was placed there for four passes,
looked plausible each time, and a single question from a playtest showed it was
meaningless. The rule is in `_dig_line` with a comment.

**One writer per surface.** The projector is painted by the event stream and
nothing else. Anything that renders from an HTTP reply will race with the
animation and look frozen.

## What has not been tested

Nearly all of the important parts, honestly.

- **No room has played this.** Every balance number comes from a scripted model
  of a room. The model does not argue, suspect anyone, or vote badly on purpose.
- **No projector has shown these maps.** They were judged on a laptop screen by
  one person. Legibility at ten metres in a lit hall is a different question and
  may reorder the styles.
- **No phone fleet has joined at once.** The server has been exercised by tests
  and by a handful of browser tabs. Twenty phones on event conferencing wifi is
  untested.
- **The game master interface has only ever been driven by its author**, who
  knows what every button does.

The next useful thing is not more code. It is one evening with real people and
somebody taking notes.
