# One reversible shot instead of three chapters

The three-scene spectral edit restored clarity, but its dark transitions still
felt like separate modules. The next edit keeps the successful compositions and
places them on one reversible camera path with no cuts and no fades.

The opening establishes the fine selected spectral signature while the camera
moves from the oblique forest view to the mapped footprint. From that view the
camera dollies outward on the same bearing and lowers into a side-on composition
as the 10 metre and 60 metre resolution planes rise. The camera then returns to
the mapped footprint while the planes descend and become transparent. The 60
metre EMIT selection strengthens beneath them. Finally the opening camera move
runs in reverse with the satellite cells attached to the forest.

The first return draft exposed a projection failure that still frames did not
show. Once a plane corner moved beyond the viewport, the old compositor dropped
the entire plane. The replacement projects off-screen corners and clips only at
the final image boundary, allowing both layers to collapse continuously.

The narrative now uses two sentences. One introduces spectral drones around the
new gaps. The other introduces satellite generalization. Layer labels, the
height and spectral legends and small source credits remain for interpretation
and evidence boundaries. No risk surface is included.
