# Less writing

The first combined final carried production notes into the viewing experience.
The gallery explained duration, transitions and editorial status before the
viewer pressed play. The film repeated color names beside color swatches and
put the opening photograph's complete license line inside the location caption.

The replacement is called `Finding fuel corridors`. Its gallery has one short
introduction, the film title and links. The supporting studies have only a name
and source links. Production reasoning remains in the repository instead of on
the viewing surface.

The film now uses `PYROCENE: FINDING FUEL CORRIDORS` as its header. The opening
shows place and date without a license paragraph. Creator, source, license and
adaptation details remain in the machine-readable manifest and documentation.
Spectral legends now contain one colored swatch and one meaning. Their glass
panels shrink because there is no empty explanatory column.

The underlying measurements, camera paths and 0.4 second matched-map join did
not change. The 88.125 second H.264 master passed a complete decode. Opening,
legend, join and ending frames were inspected at full resolution. The previous
combined final remains unchanged as an earlier checkpoint.
