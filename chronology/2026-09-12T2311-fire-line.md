# The fire line took five passes

Of everything in stage 2 the trench was the hardest to get right, and it is the
single most important object on the map, because it is the one thing the room
builds on purpose. If a room spends a night on resilience and then cannot see
what it bought, the lesson does not land.

It was wrong five times, in five different ways. Listing them because the
pattern is instructive: every version passed its tests and every version was
visibly wrong on the projector.

1. **Dug around the lantana, not around the thing being protected.** A fire
   break goes between the fuel and the asset. This one ringed the fuel, which
   is backwards and also reads as helping the fire.
2. **Fell off the map.** For clusters near an edge, the ring ran off the board
   and the drawn trench had gaps in it for no reason a viewer could see.
3. **Gave up at the river.** A greedy walk toward the fuel would reach water and
   stop, leaving a stub.
4. **Never got dug.** The forced run was queued behind the spread step and hit
   the per-night cell cap, so on busy nights the resilience choice produced
   nothing at all.
5. **Digging a trench made the fire worse.** The trench cells were being added
   to the burnable budget. The room could spend a night on protection and end up
   with a larger burn. This is the one that would have taught the opposite of
   the intended lesson.

The version that stands picks a start cell on the asset's rim nearest the fuel,
walks the rim from either end preferring cells closer to the fuel, and skips any
cell adjacent to water. Skipping water came from a direct question during a
playtest: why is there a trench along the river, is the lantana in the river? It
was not a rendering artefact. The trench really was being placed there, and a
trench against water is pointless, because the water is already the break.

Whether the fire then runs into it is a separate breadth-first search from the
ignition through burnable land, which routes around the river rather than
through it.

## Measured

Re-run on 2026-09-13 at commit `41b279c`. Forty seeds, twelve players, scripted
play. The first block digs a trench on the first resilience night; the second
plays whole games alternating between hunting and digging.

| | |
|---|---|
| Trench dug when resilience is chosen | 40/40 |
| Trench is one connected run | 40/40 |
| Any trench cell beside water | 0/40 |
| Fire reached it the same night it was dug | 14/40 |
| Fire reached it at some point in the game | 40/40 |

The middle row is the interesting one. A trench dug on night one usually does
not get tested that night, because early fires are three squares in a random
patch. It always gets tested eventually. That is the right shape: preparing for
fire means paying now for something that may not matter until later, and a
mechanic that rewarded the trench immediately every time would teach the room
to treat it as a spell rather than as preparation.
