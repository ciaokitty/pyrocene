# Beneath the canopy: cinema anchored to real measurements

The first cinematic replay looked like a fire, but its scanner and dotted front made the forest feel like a board again. The next experiment therefore removed both. It opens on the same blue-hour forest and lets the fire image do only one job: make a low fire under a living crown emotionally legible. Then the image yields to measurements.

The measurement sequence uses two ForestScan instruments at Paracou, French Guiana. An airborne 2019 scan supplies an 80 m crop over the FG5c1 plot landscape. Its crown initially looks like a solid roof; fading the returns above ten metres reveals how little of the lowest layer is obvious from above. A 2022 ground scan then moves the camera among stems and gaps. The final filter keeps returns 0.2–2 m above measured ground in magenta. All of those points are real.

That last sentence needed a boundary. Height does not identify Lantana, grass, litter, moisture or flammability. The film says so on screen, and the data-only companion ends on it: “Structure is not fuel identity.” An independent ForestScan FG6c2 tile with real terrain/leaf/wood segmentation was also acquired and checked, but even those structural labels do not turn into fuel species. A field-labelled fuel survey remains the missing dataset.

Two masters came out of the study. `Beneath the Canopy` is the 44-second emotional/evidence hybrid. `Rainforest Structure: Evidence Cut` is a 36-second all-data companion. Both are deterministic 1080p H.264 files with no audio stream. Full-resolution contact sheets and sampled frames were opened after rendering; their captions, height legend and transitions remain legible at native size.

The tempting Central Amazon fire dataset was researched but not used in these films. Its wildfire occurred in September 2015, while the repeated airborne surveys were flown in May 2017 and May 2018. It can show post-fire structural legacy, not the pre-fire fuel that caused the event; the required Amazonas archives are also roughly 35 GB each. That is a separate forensic film, not a truthful substitute for this structural study.
