# The event pair and the complete film inventory

Two films are now selected for the Codex Linux portion of the event.

First comes `pyrocene-hybrid-v2.mp4` from the port 8021 gallery. It is the
22 second cinematic companion. Generated forest plates supply atmosphere while
irregular point clusters preserve the recorded sample fire-front geometry. It
is the emotional bridge, not documentary footage and not a render of the game
that has just ended.

Second comes `pyrocene-forest-structure-and-spectra-v1.mp4` from the port 8022
gallery. It is the 88.125 second evidence film. A real fire photograph moves
into measured airborne and terrestrial LiDAR, the 2.5 year post-fire Amazon
scan, a fine educational spectral composite and real 60 metre Amazon EMIT
spectra. This is the canonical final.

The other files remain because they explain how those choices were reached.
They are not alternate event finals. The inventory below accounts for every MP4
currently retained under `/mnt/seagate/videos/pyrocene/`. Paths are relative to
that root.
