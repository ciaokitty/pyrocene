# What gets measured gets paid

19 September 2026, overnight. Branch `stage4_improvements`. Nothing committed.

The user asked for a core loop for Stage 4 out of `stage4/v2_gameloop.md`: removal
and restoration as one economy, with fire, enrichment and payment policy, fun
alone and usable in a room. Design is in `stage4/v1_policy_gaming.md`. The
accepted Cooperation / Negligence game is untouched. The new game is its own
route, `/policy.html`, on the same point cloud.

## Order of work

Model first, no screen. A deterministic season loop on six patches, three
scripted crews and a beam search for the ceiling. Tuned until the crews lost in
different ways by the tonne and by the tree, and a paced crew held a forest
under both. Then mission 1 on screen, played click by click with Playwright and
read from screenshots, before mission 2 or 3 existed on screen. Then the animals,
the ten-years-on playback, the funder ending and the harder game, each played
before the next.

## What went wrong on the way

The first economy allowed one forest in ten seasons at best. The first fire
schedule burned a first planting before the player could have known. A
forest-first opening went bankrupt in season two. Bare ground had a one-season
planting window. Hazel called three times in one season. The animals were drawn
and could not be seen. The result card hid the island. The first money-following
crew never planted under any rule because its search discarded plantings. Each
is recorded with its fix in the design file.

A blind scripted line is not a playtest. Twice a fixed list of clicks fell out of
step with the game and told me nothing. The terminal bench, which prints the
board after every move, is how the real decisions were tried.

## Checks

Ten model tests, five browser playthroughs including the funder ending under two
rules and a phone-sized screen, the existing round and neglect model tests, and
the delivery tests all pass. The browser test file was written by the Codex CLI
from a written brief inside its workspace sandbox; it changed no other file.
The offline package was not rebuilt. No room has played this.
