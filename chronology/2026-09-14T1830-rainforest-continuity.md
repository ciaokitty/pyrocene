# Keeping the evidence in one rainforest

The first hyperspectral studies failed editorially. Their measurements were
real, but the cut left humid tropical rainforest for dry deciduous landscapes.
The visual grammar changed at the same moment as the data format. The audience
would have had to decide whether they were still watching the same investigation.
We retired those videos from the active gallery and kept their files in a
recoverable archive.

The replacement keeps the frozen LiDAR film unchanged and builds three short
modules around tropical rainforest evidence. The renderer, captions and claim
contract live in
[`stage2/simulation/codex/rainforest_continuity/`](../stage2/simulation/codex/rainforest_continuity/).

## Structure that is not uniform

The Nouragues source search produced more than a generic rainforest scan. Dryad
contains the mapped 2012 liana-infested zone and a canopy-height model. CEDA
contains open 2019 airborne LiDAR. One downloaded airborne tile barely overlaps
the mapped zone and another lies mainly inside it. The film compares those
source returns with the date mismatch visible in the location line, then moves
into a published terrestrial LiDAR liana-classification figure.

This supports a narrow claim: liana-associated rainforest structure can be
located and liana stems can be classified in terrestrial scans. It does not
support treating every lower return as liana, invasive vegetation or fuel.

## Structure to composition

A 2025 Hawaiian wet-forest study published terrestrial LiDAR cross-sections for
low, moderate and high strawberry guava abundance. They supply the missing
literal invasive example without changing biome. A separate 2008 Hawaiian
study supplies the imaging-spectroscopy classification. The film gives both the
same cyan, green and magenta grammar, while its manifest states that the sources
are separate studies and not spatially registered.

## A mechanism without a false map

The third module keeps measured Nouragues returns on screen and adds an amber
light field at a canopy opening. Published sensor work supports the sentence
that gaps can be hotter and less humid. The light itself is deliberately logged
as explanatory. It is not a reconstructed temperature, humidity or fuel-moisture
measurement.

## What changed in the pipeline

Every word remains editable in one JSON file. The renderer rejects the visual
punctuation already removed from the frozen LiDAR film. Each output has a source
manifest, and every candidate stays separate from the frozen master until an
editorial choice is made. The old candidate record remains in version control
with a retired status so the project history is not rewritten.
