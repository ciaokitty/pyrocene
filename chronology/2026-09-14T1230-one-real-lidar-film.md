# One real photograph and three measured point clouds

The first hybrid mixed generated rainforest plates with real LiDAR. That weakened the evidence: once the opening looked synthetic, the measured point cloud could be mistaken for another generated image. The next cut removed the generated plates and the three-film comparison from the review flow.

The replacement is one 56-second silent film. It starts with Erick Caldas Xavier's real photograph of an active 2019 fire in Iquiri National Forest. A slow camera crop dissolves into the Paracou airborne cloud. The camera enters the Paracou terrestrial scan, progressively reveals the measured 0.2–2 m layer, pulls back, and moves into the EBA `T_0638` scan acquired 2.5 years after the documented Autazes fire.

The source changes are not hidden. Each location types itself at the bottom of the frame. Iquiri, Paracou and Autazes are separate observations assembled into an explanatory sequence, not a registered before-and-after claim.

The interface is now limited to four things:

- `PYROCENE: FOREST STRUCTURE STUDY`;
- the measured-height legend;
- one typed location line; and
- one short sentence at a time in a compact blurred panel.

The last sentence is the evidence boundary: “Forest structure alone does not identify fuel.” Every displayed word is in one JSON file and one command regenerates only this film.
