# Before the vigilance experiment

The main game remains Expedition → Cooperation → Negligence. The user prefers
its layout and negotiation mechanics. Ledger and Prelude are disposable
learning environments, not their replacement.

Checkpoint `769b21c` before tonight's work. New risks, purchases and player
experiments belong in `stage4/labs/vigilance/`. Do not change the accepted
round model, shared-room protocol, species records or point-cloud renderer.
Keep the existing Ledger and Prelude playable too.

Tonight tests capital spent on information and protection while restoration
remains vulnerable. Seeded variation must be explainable: repeated inspection
or undo must not reroll weather, spread or fire. Players need honest feedback
about why a canopy did not close. Sensors disclose evidence; they must not
silently make plants safer. Protection has an explicit cost and duration.

Use lower-cost Claude and Cursor CLI players with contrasting priorities,
without showing them hidden model state. Record losing strategies as well as
successful ones. Document how useful pieces can later be adapted to the real
two-team game, only after the user's review.
