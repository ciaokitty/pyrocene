# One end-to-end film

The LiDAR film ended on the same Amazon point-cloud map and almost exactly the
same camera bearing that opened the approved spectral continuation. Adding a
new rotation would have made the geography less stable rather than making the
join more cinematic.

The final assembly therefore uses a 0.4 second matched-map dissolve beginning
at 55.6 seconds. The last LiDAR sentence dissolves completely before the drone
spectral sentence appears. The point cloud remains spatially locked through the
join. From there the existing reversible spectral move continues uninterrupted
through the resolution stack and the 60 metre EMIT view.

The result is one silent 88.125 second film at 1920 by 1080 and 24 frames per
second. A complete decode passed. A full contact sheet checked the narrative
from the opening fire photograph to the satellite cells, and a dense join sheet
checked the transition at tenth-second intervals. The two source masters remain
unchanged.
