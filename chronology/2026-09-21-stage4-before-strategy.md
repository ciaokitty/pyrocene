# Preserve the accepted game before Strategy

The accepted compact Cooperation / Negligence game is committed at `f9b7511`.
The tag `stage4-before-strategy` preserves that exact code. Its behaviour and
UI are recorded in [the previous entry](2026-09-20-stage4-compact-round-panels.md).

The next stage is **Strategy**, not Vigilance. It becomes a local single-player
experiment: each player controls removal and restoration. Canopy-filled ledger
blocks track unfinished care. A plot leaves after closure or after reinvasion,
with distinct outcomes. Player work is followed by an occasional wildfire phase.
Fire damage is the outcome to watch, not another abstract forest-health score.

Three suggested approaches are Rush, One at a time, and Anchor. These are advice,
not difficulty modifiers. The same seed must expose the same underlying weather
to each approach. Earlier lab success rates are not measurements of this engine.
No shops, contracts, sensors or new protection buttons in this first pass.

Shared Cooperation/Negligence authority, budgets and proposals remain intact.
Strategy forks the committed landscape into each player's own run. It must not
write its actions back to a room. Live rooms on 8024 should not be lost during
deployment. Lower-model players will test the new engine; browser play and
screenshots will test the actual point-cloud UI before handoff.

`stage4/v2_strategies.md` was present as an untracked user-authored brainstorm at
the start. It has been read, but will not be edited or silently included in this
checkpoint. The current request takes precedence over its extra proposed tools.
