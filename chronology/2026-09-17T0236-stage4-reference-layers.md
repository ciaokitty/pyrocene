# Communities and wildlife in the same map

The readable-structure checkpoint is `ac54772`. This continuation keeps that
map and adds a single Explore selector, not a new dashboard or mission chain.
Communities, Audio and Camera traps open their reference locations overhead.
Selecting a marker reveals the record in the existing square. Forest reverses
the reveal. Plants returns to free exploration.

The community view uses 49 real Microsoft building footprints near Alter do
Chao, Para. Only the footprints are measured/inferred data. Extruded walls and
the neighbour/harvest account are declared illustrations. We do not infer fire
risk, ownership or wrongdoing from houses.

The listening post plays a licensed British Library screaming-piha recording
from Peru and shows a same-species photograph from Manaus. Its waveform is
computed from the actual recording. The camera layer uses labelled WCS frames
of a lowland tapir, jaguar and white-lipped peccary from Bolivia. SA-FARI was
considered, but downloading it requires approved access. No restriction was
bypassed. Camera images are references, not observations at these map squares.

The field-terminal treatment is green with scanlines. It preserves photo
proportions. Wildlife is not presented as reconstructed 3D geometry. Several
blurred and clipped jaguar frames were rejected after visual inspection. The
chosen resting-animal frame retains its original timestamp. Raw trial media
were moved to the external QA directory and remain recoverable.

Browser iteration also found a layer-switch/camera-motion race and a phone
selector covering a map marker. Both were corrected. Audio pauses on departure,
dialog entry and page hiding. Photo and community references no longer fetch
unrelated TLS assets. The portable archive includes the selected media, source
metadata, hashes and license copies. There are no runtime remote requests.

Verification commands: `stage4.test_inline`, `stage4.test_observations`,
`stage4.test_expedition`, `stage4.test_delivery`, `stage4.test_portable`, and the
expedition-state, living-landscape and memory-model Node tests. Screenshot
artifacts include `inline-plant-note.png`, `observations-community.png`,
`observations-audio.png`, `observations-camera-21.png`, `observations-phone.png`
and the corresponding disabled-WebGL captures. These live under the external
Stage 4 `qa-expedition` directory.

This is a deliberately small reference set. It is not a live acoustic classifier,
a population survey or a new physically registered multi-sensor dataset. Actual
team-laptop performance and whether the revised plant structure is legible enough
still need user feedback. The three supplemental tree scans have no confirmed
botanical identity. Species photos provide the independent identification aid.
