# Balance we decided not to fake

Stage 2 gives the room one choice a night: hunt lantana, or spend the night on
fire resilience. For that to be a real choice, neither should dominate.

Neither strategy was tuned into parity, and this is a deliberate decision that
should be written down before somebody "fixes" it later.

## The numbers

300 games per strategy, 12 players, scripted play at skill 0.35. Re-run on
2026-09-13 at commit `41b279c` for this entry.

Three strategies: **hunter** always votes lantana, **turtle** always takes
resilience, **warden** alternates by reading the board.

| | win rate | rounds | end health | village losses | fire losses |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| hunter | 59.7% | 4.9 | 57.3% | 59 | 10 |
| warden | 58.3% | 5.2 | 55.5% | 24 | 16 |
| turtle | 0% | 6.9 | 50.3% | 21 | 18 |

Turtle's 0% is not a bug. The only win condition is removing every lantana
player, so a room that never votes cannot win by construction. 249 of its 300
losses are the night-8 timeout, and the rest are the forest or the village going
under first. That is the lesson the strategy exists to deliver: shelter
indefinitely and you survive at half health and still lose.

Hunting and warding come out close. Hunting dominates the middle of the game for
an honest reason: removing a lantana player removes an entire stand of fuel
permanently, which lowers fire severity for every remaining night. Resilience
only ever buys one night, except the trench, which persists.

## What was tried and rejected

Several knobs would have flattened the curve. Making removal leave the ground
infested, weakening the link between stand size and severity, making the trench
cheaper. Each of them works, and each of them lies about the ecology. The
reason hunting is strong in the model is the reason source control is strong in
the field, and a game that hides that to feel balanced is teaching the wrong
thing.

What was added instead is a rule with real teeth: fire reaching the homes ends
the game immediately. That is why hunter loses 59 games to the village and
warden loses 24. A pure hunter accumulates risk it never pays down, and
eventually a fire gets to the houses. The strategies converge on win rate and
differ completely in how they lose, which is a better teaching outcome than
equal win rates would have been.

## Limits

This is a model of a room, not a room. Scripted play at a fixed skill level does
not argue, get suspicious of the quiet person, or vote badly on purpose. The
numbers are a sanity check on the mechanics, not a prediction of what happens on
the night. Nothing here has been played with people yet.
