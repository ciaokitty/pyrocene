# Everything Ember says on the projector. Grep a line to change it.
# {n} number of squares, {dir} a compass direction, {r} a round number,
# {asset} what a fire line protects, {level} a forecast word, {wind} a direction.

# --- the round opens ---------------------------------------------------------
[round.open]
Night {r}.

# --- eliminations, as the map shows them --------------------------------------
[elim.lantana]
A lantana patch in the {dir} was found and pulled out. Bare ground for now.

[elim.lantana_native]
A lantana patch in the {dir} was found and pulled out. Native forest takes it back.

[elim.native]
A native stand in the {dir} was lost. Lantana moves in.

[elim.ecologist]
The ecologist is gone. The map does not change. The room just got harder to read.

[elim.ranger]
The ranger is gone. The map does not change. Nobody is saving anyone now.

# --- resilience ---------------------------------------------------------------
[line.placed]
A fire line is dug on the {dir} side of the dense lantana. It shields the {asset}.

[line.none]
There is no dense lantana to fence yet. The crew digs a line on the {dir} side of the biggest patch anyway.

[water.ready]
A response team is on standby tonight. Whatever starts, they will get to it fast.

[forecast]
Forecast for next night: {level}. Wind from the {wind}.

[forecast.none]
no fire

[forecast.small]
a small fire

[forecast.medium]
a fire that will run

[forecast.large]
a big fire

# --- why the system picked what it picked ----------------------------------------
[auto.village]

[auto.native]
The dense lantana borders native forest, so the crew digs a fire line.

[auto.big]
The fuel load is high enough for a big fire tonight, so a response team stands by.

[auto.watch]

# --- growth ------------------------------------------------------------------
[growth]
Lantana spread into {n} more squares.

[growth.one]
Lantana spread into one more square.

[growth.none]
Lantana did not spread tonight.

# --- the turn ------------------------------------------------------------------
# Said once, the night separate patches become one run of fuel. Everything the
# room does from here is different, so it gets its own card and its own animation.

[network]
The lantana now runs in one connected band across {n} patches. Fire can carry the whole length of it. Taking one patch out no longer breaks the chain.

# --- fire ---------------------------------------------------------------------
[fire.quiet]

[fire.spark]

[fire.spark.one]

[fire.sev2]

[fire.sev3]

[fire.water]

[fire.blocked]

[fire.village]

[fire.cost]

# --- endings ------------------------------------------------------------------

[end.lose.time]
