# Research brief: real fire and forward model

Copy this into an online research chatbot first. Once the case and model have
been challenged and selected, give the conversation and source bundle to a CLI
agent for implementation.

> I am producing a cinematic post-game wildfire film for Pyrocene, a
> participatory game about invasive lantana, accumulating fuel, delayed
> collective action, ignition, spread and resilience. Work on two separate,
> evidence-led tracks.
>
> **Track A: a forensic real-fire case.** Find one well-documented fire where
> *Lantana camara*, or a better-supported comparable invasive shrub or unusually
> continuous surface-fuel load, materially affected spread or severity. Do not
> call vegetation the ignition source unless evidence says it was. Reconstruct
> the pre-fire landscape, verified ignition location and time, fuel history,
> weather and wind, timestamped spread/perimeters, impacts and interventions.
> Separate established facts, expert interpretation and uncertainty. Prefer
> official incident reports, peer-reviewed work, satellite or airborne products
> and downloadable spatial data. Reject cases supported only by news repetition
> or a final burn scar.
>
> **Track B: a reproducible forward simulation.** Find and test a credible model
> that can turn Pyrocene’s final 22 by 12 board into a clearly labeled modeled
> scenario. Evaluate Google Research’s wildfire work and WindTL, but verify
> whether either is actually runnable; challenge the current leading candidate,
> ForeFire 2.5, against Cell2Fire-World, ELMFIRE, FlamMap/FARSITE, QUIC-Fire and
> other defensible options. Specify how native forest, three lantana stages,
> bare ground, hills, water, roads, villages and fire lines map into physical
> fuels, DEM, barriers, ignition, wind and moisture. Require time-stepped fronts
> or arrival rasters, licensing, compute requirements, a pinned command or
> container, uncertainty runs and scientific limitations. Never describe
> categorical game cells as calibrated fuel beds without measurements.
>
> A candidate real fire is acceptable only if it has a known ignition, pre-fire
> fuel/land-cover and elevation, time-resolved weather, and at least three
> independent timestamped perimeters or thermal observations. Calibrate on the
> ignition and earliest perimeter, freeze parameters, then validate on later
> perimeters using arrival-time error, perimeter overlap and burned-area error.
> Begin by asking me for the event constraints and board schema. Deliver the
> best case and best simulator separately, with primary-source links, an asset
> inventory, claim boundaries and a practical implementation plan.

## Non-negotiable handoff fields

Every proposed source or artifact must include provenance, license, retrieval
date, stable URL, local filename, checksum, coordinate reference system when
spatial, temporal resolution, and the claims it can and cannot support.
